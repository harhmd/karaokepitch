Singing in Your Vocal Comfort Zone

🎵 Male to Female (+3 to +5)

If a woman is singing a male original, raising it 3 to 5 semitones usually places it directly into comfortable mezzo/soprano tessitura without forcing an octave jump.

🎵 Female to Male (−3 to −5)

If a man is singing a female pop track, shifting it 3 to 5 semitones down lowers the high chorus notes into comfortable baritone or tenor chest voice.

🎙️ Vocal Reduction

Toggle Vocal Reduce in More Options. It separates mid/side stereo channels to cancel the centered lead vocal while preserving stereo instruments and bass.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does shifting pitch change the duration or speed of my song?

No. The phase vocoder and time-stretching algorithms maintain the tempo 100% steady while transposing musical frequencies. If you do want to speed up or slow down the song, you can use the independent Tempo control.

Are files uploaded to an external server?

Never. KaraokePitch performs all audio decoding, key detection, pitch shifting, and MP3/WAV encoding strictly inside your browser using the HTML5 Web Audio API and Web Workers. Your media never leaves your device.

What is Formant Preservation?

When you shift vocals without formant preservation, the singer's vocal tract length is artificially scaled, causing high notes to sound like a chipmunk and low notes to sound unnaturally deep. Formant preservation keeps the acoustic resonances of the throat and mouth untouched for natural-sounding vocals.

How does Vocal Reduction work?

Most commercial music is mixed with the lead vocal panned dead-center, while drums, synthesizers, and guitars are spread across stereo channels. Vocal reduction inverts phase between left and right channels while applying a low-pass crossover to keep bass and kick intact.

Which audio formats are supported?

KaraokePitch opens MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, FLAC, and can extract audio directly from MP4, MOV, and WebM video files.
